Two things worth knowing before you trust it

The silence list is now the only thing that silences anything. It used to be a formality — unnamed conditions were thrown away regardless. Now it is the only exit to silence, which makes adding to it a real decision, and an easy one to make casually while clearing a junk ticket.

Expect some junk tickets for a few weeks. The panels will say things nobody has named, and each one now gets a ticket. That is the mechanism working, not misfiring, and every junk ticket is one decision away from never appearing again. It is the trade we picked: noise you can see, over silence you cannot.
